Colour

Purple is the voice of the brand. Use deep purples for authority (headings, hero panels), light tints for containers and cards, and gold sparingly as an accent — never as a primary surface.

Typography

A chunky slab-serif for display & headings sets the tone (confident, grounded, trustworthy). A humanist sans-serif carries all body text. Numbers are set in the body face at heavier weights for a clean, legible feel.

Spacing, radius & elevation

A 4px baseline grid. Radius is generous — everything has rounded corners. Elevation is restrained: one resting shadow and one lifted shadow; no nested shadows.

Iconography

Line icons, 1.75px stroke, rounded caps & joins, single purple colour. Always inside a tinted rounded square container (36–44px). No filled, coloured, or multi-tone icons.

Do

Use a 36x36 tinted square container with a 26px line icon inside.

Dimensions

Container 36 × 36 · radius 6 · bg purple.050 · icon stroke purple.700

Don't

Avoid filled icons, mixed stroke weights, or colours other than purple.700.

Charts

Charts use the purple scale as an ordinal palette. The most recent or headline data point is rendered in purple.700; historical values step down through purple.300 → purple.200. Axes are light grey; gridlines are omitted where possible.

Voice & tone

Confident

"#1 UK Private Bank and Wealth Manager" · "Leading position" · "Material synergies"

Evidence-led

Every claim has a number, a percentage, or a footnote. Headings state conclusions; bullets carry the data.

Plain-spoken

Short declarative sentences. Industry terms are defined on first use (AUMA, CAL, RoTE).

Do & don't

Do lead every slide with a single-sentence takeaway (the H2). Keep titles under 12 words.
Do give every metric a label and a source footnote superscript.
Do use arrow-tail labels to pair a category with its evidence.
Do round corners generously — radius.md is the default.
Don't stack more than 5 items in a statement stack; split across slides instead.
Don't use gold as a text colour or as a container background.
Don't mix sans-serif bold with slab for a heading — pick one per headline.
Don't use filled icons or multi-colour illustration — line only, purple only.
