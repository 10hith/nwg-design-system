# s-process — Preset Recipe

**Components used:** title-block + 3× step card (date + title + body + image placeholder) + 3× action-item strip

**Use verbatim when:** exactly 3 time-ordered steps with dates + an NWG-actions row at the bottom

**Deviate (use Mode A) when:** 4+ steps, no dates, steps of very different lengths, or you want a horizontal timeline with milestone markers
