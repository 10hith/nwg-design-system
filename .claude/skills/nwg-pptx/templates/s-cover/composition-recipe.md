# s-cover — Preset Recipe

**Components used:** hero-band (full-slide) + arc-accent (decorative) + kicker-label + title-block + date text

**Use verbatim when:** opening slide with a clear title, subtitle, and date — no data, no body content

**Deviate (use Mode A) when:** you want a split-panel cover, a large background image, an editorial typographic treatment, or anything beyond title+subtitle+date
