# s-chart — Preset Recipe

**Components used:** grid-2col (chart left + narrative right) + inline-chart + kicker-label + title-block + bullet-row (narrative items)

**Use verbatim when:** one chart + 2–4 contextual bullet points, standard bar/line/area chart type

**Deviate (use Mode A) when:** you want the chart to dominate full-slide, you need a custom chart style, or the narrative is longer than 4 bullets
