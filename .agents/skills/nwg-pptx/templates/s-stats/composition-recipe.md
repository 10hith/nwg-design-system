# s-stats — Preset Recipe

**Components used:** title-block + divider-bar (group labels) + 2× stat-row (4 stat-tiles each)

**Use verbatim when:** 4–8 independent KPIs grouped into exactly 2 named sets

**Deviate (use Mode A) when:** you want editorial-scale numbers (1–2 hero stats dominating the slide), mixing stat-tiles with supporting chart or narrative, or more than 2 groups
