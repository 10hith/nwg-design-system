# s-agenda — Preset Recipe

**Components used:** grid-2col (presenter panel left + agenda list right) + icon-pill (avatar circles) + divider-bar (agenda item separators)

**Use verbatim when:** exactly 2–4 presenters + a numbered agenda list with no other content

**Deviate (use Mode A) when:** no presenters, single agenda column, or you want to combine agenda with a visual timeline
