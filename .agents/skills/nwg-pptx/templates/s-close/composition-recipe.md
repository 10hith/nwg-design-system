# s-close — Preset Recipe

**Components used:** hero-band (full-slide dark) + large title + gold subtitle + 2-column prose paragraphs

**Use verbatim when:** a standard "Thank you / Q&A" closing slide with optional contact or next-steps paragraphs

**Deviate (use Mode A) when:** you want a closing slide with a visual, a stat, a contact card grid, or any non-prose content
